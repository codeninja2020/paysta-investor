Getting African creators paid fast.

Paysta turns US-dollar income from streaming royalties and PayPal into money in a South African bank account or e-wallet. It runs on the web and on WhatsApp.

01
The problem

Creators earn in dollars, but they live in rand.

Payments take 4–7 days

Withdrawals from distributors and PayPal take four to seven days to reach a local account. Creators who need the money now have to wait, or turn to costly informal options.

Royalties stuck abroad

Independent artists are paid by TuneCore, Ditto and CD Baby in US dollars. Withdrawals need foreign accounts, Payoneer or PayPal, and each hop adds days and fees.

PayPal is hard to cash out

Freelancers and creators get paid in PayPal dollars, but turning that balance into rand in their own bank account or e-wallet is slow and confusing.

Disconnected payment systems

Mobile money and local banks aren't linked to global payment platforms like PayPal, TuneCore and Payoneer. Money can't flow straight from where creators earn to where they spend, so every payout needs workarounds.

02
The solution

Two products, one payout engine.

Paysta pays creators first, in rand, to their bank or mobile money. We then take on the 4–7 day wait and the complicated processing ourselves, across distributors, PayPal and local banks.

app.paysta.co.za

Paysta Royalties

Artists see their balance and request an instant payout, so there's no 4–7 day wait. They can also choose a standard payout (7–10 days) or an advance against future royalties. Paysta withdraws from the distributor and pays rand to their bank or mobile money, with a PDF invoice for every payout.

payments.paysta.co.zaWhatsApp

Paysta Daily: PayPal payouts

Customers get an instant rand quote, pay through PayPal Checkout and receive rand in their FNB, Capitec or other bank account, or an e-wallet. The whole flow also runs inside WhatsApp, with an AI assistant and saved payout details.

Product

Built to run at scale, without a call centre.

WhatsApp-native

AI assistant

09
Business model

We earn on every dollar that moves.

Royalty payout fees

A fee on each withdrawal, with higher fees for instant payouts and advances. The historical take rate is 7.8%.

FX spread on PayPal payouts

The customer sees one rand amount, and Paysta keeps the margin between the live dollar rate and that quote.

Advances

Royalty advances are paid ahead of distributor payouts and recovered from future royalties, priced as a premium payout.

10
What's next

From payouts to a creator wallet.

Each step makes payouts faster and brings in more creators per rand spent.

  1. Instant rand settlement: automate bank payouts the moment PayPal confirms, using PayShap.
  2. More distributors: direct balance sync from TuneCore, Ditto and CD Baby, so artists see what they can withdraw today.
  3. Data-driven advances: size advances from each artist's earnings history across Apple, Spotify and YouTube.
  4. Grow on WhatsApp: referrals, agents and other languages, all handled by the AI assistant.
  5. Regional expansion: Zimbabwe and other SADC payout corridors.
